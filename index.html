Other people's records were one number away.

Affected
UUCMS, a Karnataka government website. Similar flaws on college and medical websites in Kerala: kept private
Class
Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR), filed under Broken Access Control in the OWASP Top 10.
Impact
A logged-in user could reach other people's records by changing an ID, which can expose sensitive database information.
What I did
Found it with Burp Suite and Kali Linux, then reported it instead of using it.
The fix
Check on the server, for every request, that the record belongs to the person asking.

The sites' details stay hidden because responsible disclosure means protecting the people whose data was at risk. To show how the bug works without touching a real site, here is a small made-up version you can break yourself.

Reproduce it (made-up data)Logged in as Asha, invoice 1042
